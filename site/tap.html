Case · Transport & logistics

TAP Air Portugal

More cash than bank debt, yet €2,293m of net debt (3.16x EBITDA) once the leased aircraft (IFRS 16) and pensions are counted.

Consolidated accounts FY2023 to FY2025, 2025 Integrated Report and 2024 annual report · 2025 Integrated Report, pp. 152–156, notes 10 and 20

Key insights

Three points for the first call

Each with the figure, why it matters in a deal and the question for management.

Net debt is a definition, not a number

Borrowings less cash are -€43m in FY25: cash exceeds bank borrowings. Adjusted net debt is €2,293m, 3.16x EBITDA, once aircraft leases (IFRS 16) and pensions are counted. The company's own net debt in note 19 is €817m, because it leaves out the leases without purchase option.

Enterprise value to equity deducts net debt. Which leases, pensions and provisions count is negotiated in the SPA, and a few hundred million move between buyer and seller.

“Which net debt definition do the facility agreements and the covenants use?”

EBITDA under IFRS 16 is not comparable with pre-IFRS 16 multiples

Reported EBITDA is €726m in FY25. Less the lease payments of €464m, it is €262m.

An EV/EBITDA multiple must use EBITDA and net debt on the same basis. Leases are either in both or in neither.

“How is the fleet financed (leases, renting), and is it all in reported debt?”

The notes turn "provisions" into a debt-like question

Provisions are €415m at the end of 2025. Note 18 says €291m of it is aircraft redelivery costs and €78m is legal claims.

Redelivery costs are cash that leaves when a leased aircraft goes back. A buyer argues they are debt-like; a seller argues they are operating.

“What is the schedule of aircraft redeliveries and the cash cost by year?”

How it is computed

Score = min(100, 100 × points / 30); points per open issue = severity (High 3, Medium 2, Low 1) × materiality (material 1, not quantifiable 0.75, below materiality 0.5) × evidence (open 1, partly explained by a note 0.75, explained 0.5). Issues = the findings and the balance sheet items still to classify (Medium); items already in adjusted net debt are priced, not scored. 100 = ten open, material High findings.
