How it works

Five steps, the order a deal team follows

The code does every calculation and raises every finding. AI only writes the memo, and a checker rejects any figure it cannot trace.

  1. Read the accountsEvery line of the statements, with its sheet and cell
  2. Check the readingRebuild the published subtotals before trusting a figure
  3. Flag what matters30 rules, each with evidence, source and a question
  4. Build the bridgesEBITDA, net debt and working capital, as a deal team asks
  5. Export to ExcelA databook with live formulas and every source

Limits

What it does not do

  • Annual accounts only (no monthly figures, LTM or budget).
  • Euros only.
  • Financial due diligence only (no legal, tax, commercial or IT review).
  • A first pass for an analyst to review, not a conclusion.

Not measured

What is not proven yet

  • Time saved against a manual review: not measured.
  • Distortions nobody thought of, smaller ones, or several at once: the benchmark plants one known distortion at a time.
  • Review by a practitioner: the classification of the findings is proposed by the agent and not yet reviewed by a person.
