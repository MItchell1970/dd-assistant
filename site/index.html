1 · Each open issue gets points

Severity (High 3, Medium 2, Low 1) × materiality (material 1, not quantifiable 0.75, below 0.5) × evidence (open 1, partly explained by a note 0.75, explained 0.5).

2 · Only the latest year counts

A finding of an earlier year only is historical: kept, not scored. Items a buyer prices anyway (leases, the peg) sit apart.

3 · 30 points = 100

Ten open, material High findings. 0 = nothing open. A to-do meter for the diligence, not a grade for the company. TAP: 12 points from 8 open issues of FY25 = 40 / 100.
