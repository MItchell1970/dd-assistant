## Detection benchmark by mutation

Generated by `scripts/detection_benchmark.py`. One known distortion at a time is planted in a copy of the published figures of the three demo companies (three years, each with its sector and country), and the engine is run. The expected rule of each mutation was written before the first run and no threshold was changed afterwards. The published files are never changed.
**Detected: 48 of 49 planted distortions (98%).** Missed: 1. Left out of the rate: 8 where the rule already fired on the real accounts, and 0 where the accounts lack the line. Other rules that newly fired alongside: 11. Controls below materiality by design: 6 of 6 raised no High or Medium finding.
What this measures: whether a distortion of this size, planted in real accounts, trips the rule built for it. What it does not measure: distortions nobody thought of, smaller ones, or several at once.

Every run and every miss: [`docs/DETECTION.md`](DETECTION.md).
