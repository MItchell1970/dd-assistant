# TAP Air Portugal (Transportes Aéreos Portugueses, S.A.) — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · M&A buy-side · Amounts in euros, millions (€m) · Sources: published consolidated accounts FY23 to FY25 and the notes of the 2025 Integrated Report (Sheet!Cell, note and page references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of TAP, a Portuguese airline group, in the transport and logistics sector. Scope: the consolidated IFRS statements for FY23 to FY25, from the 2025 Integrated Report and the 2024 annual report. Ten notes of the 2025 report were read. All three reading checks passed: operating profit is rebuilt to the euro.

### Executive summary

1. **Net debt depends on the definition (deal view, R22, R11).** Borrowings less cash are -€43m in FY25: cash exceeds bank borrowings. With aircraft leases and pensions, adjusted net debt is €2,293m, 3.16x EBITDA. Note 19 explains the leases.
2. **EBITDA has fallen for two years and tax absorbed the profit (R25).** Reported EBITDA was €832m in FY23, €807m in FY24 and €726m in FY25. The effective tax rate was 96.9% in FY25, against 22.5% statutory.
3. **Working capital is negative and partly unexplained (R28, R07).** Net working capital is -€598m in FY25. "Other" current liabilities, net of items classified elsewhere, are €631m, and receivables grew 21.7% against revenue growth of 1.2%.
4. **Provisions and contingencies are large; the 2024 balance sheet was restated (R30).** Provisions are €415m in FY25, mostly aircraft redelivery costs (note 18). Total assets of FY24 moved from €5,739m to €5,797m between the two reports.
5. **Cash generation weakened as investment recovered (R24).** Cash conversion was 121.8% in FY23, 81.8% in FY24 and 76.6% in FY25. Seven key documents are missing (R18).

### Information quality and limitations

The figures are the published consolidated accounts, read from the English translations; the Portuguese originals prevail. FY23 comes from the 2024 report; for FY24 the comparative of the 2025 report is used. Ten notes of the 2025 report were read; the tax and pension notes were not. Year-end balances only.
