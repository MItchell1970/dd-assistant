# TAP Air Portugal (Transportes Aéreos Portugueses, S.A.) — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · M&A buy-side · Amounts in euros, millions (€m) · Sources: published consolidated accounts FY25 with FY24 comparatives (Sheet!Cell references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of TAP, a Portuguese airline group, in the transport and logistics sector. Scope: the consolidated FY24 and FY25 statements of the 2025 Integrated Report, under IFRS, with notes 10 and 20. Both reading checks passed: operating profit is rebuilt to the euro.

### Executive summary

1. **Net debt depends on the definition (deal view, R22).** Borrowings less cash are -€43m in FY25: cash exceeds bank borrowings. With aircraft leases and pensions, adjusted net debt is €2,293m, 3.16x EBITDA.
2. **EBITDA fell and almost no profit reached shareholders (R25).** Reported EBITDA fell from €807m in FY24 to €726m in FY25. The effective tax rate was 96.9% in FY25, against 22.5% statutory.
3. **Working capital is negative and partly unexplained (R07, R28).** Net working capital is -€598m in FY25. Receivables grew 21.7% against revenue growth of 1.2%, and "other" current liabilities total €1,727m.
4. **Cash generation weakened with higher capex.** Cash conversion fell from 81.8% in FY24 to 76.6% in FY25. Free cash flow fell from €298m in FY24 to €192m in FY25.
5. **Information gaps limit verification (R18).** Seven key documents are missing, including the debt schedule, the facility agreements and the AR ageing.

### Theme 5 — Information gaps (R18)

Seven key documents were not provided. They include sales by customer, the breakdown of other income and expenses, the AR ageing and the debt schedule. Without them, R07, R22 and R28 can be neither confirmed nor ruled out.

### Information quality and limitations

The figures are the published consolidated accounts, read from the English translation; the Portuguese original prevails. Only notes 10 and 20 were reviewed. Year-end balances only: there are no monthly figures. Sector benchmarks come from 56 European listed transport groups and are orientation only.

### Next steps

1. Obtain the debt schedule, the lease register and the facility agreements, and agree the net debt definition.
2. Obtain the breakdown of other current assets and liabilities and the monthly balance sheets.
3. Obtain the tax rate reconciliation and the deferred tax movements.
