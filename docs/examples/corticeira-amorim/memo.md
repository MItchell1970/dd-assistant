# Corticeira Amorim, S.G.P.S., S.A. — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · M&A buy-side · Amounts in EUR thousands (€k) · Sources: published consolidated accounts FY25 with FY24 comparatives (Sheet!Cell references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of Corticeira Amorim, a Portuguese listed group in materials and packaging (cork). Scope: the consolidated FY24 and FY25 statements of the 2025 annual report, under IFRS, in Portuguese, with note 19 on cash. The cash flow statement uses the direct method. Both reading checks passed.

### Executive summary

1. **Revenue fell, but cash generation was strong (no rule fired).** Revenue fell 8.3% to €860,967k in FY25. Operating cash flow rose to €219,015k, 155.4% of EBITDA, as inventories fell.
2. **Debt was cut and refinanced (R11).** Debt due within 12 months fell from 56.3% in FY24 to 28.7% in FY25. Adjusted net debt fell to €79,657k.
3. **Investment runs below depreciation (R24).** Capex was 0.72x D&A in FY24 and 0.71x in FY25. Maintenance needs are not disclosed.
4. **EBITDA includes other income (R04).** Other income was €17,421k in FY25, 12.4% of EBITDA. EBITDA fell from €157,566k in FY24 to €140,983k in FY25.
5. **Working capital is large and documents are missing (R18).** Net working capital is €439,589k, 51.1% of revenue in FY25. Seven key documents were not provided.

### Information quality and limitations

The figures are the published consolidated accounts, in Portuguese; only note 19 was reviewed. The published EBITDA is €140,985k; the lines read rebuild €140,983k, a rounding difference within the reading check tolerance. Year-end balances only. Benchmarks come from 42 listed European packaging groups and serve as orientation only.

### Next steps

1. Obtain monthly working capital balances and the inventory ageing by stage.
2. Obtain the debt schedule, the facility agreements and the classification of other financial liabilities.
3. Obtain the capex plan and the breakdown of other income and other operating receipts.
