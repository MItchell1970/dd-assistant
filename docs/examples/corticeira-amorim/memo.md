# Corticeira Amorim, S.G.P.S., S.A. — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · M&A buy-side · Amounts in EUR thousands (€k) · Sources: published consolidated accounts FY23 to FY25 and the notes of the 2025 annual report (Sheet!Cell and note references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of Corticeira Amorim, a Portuguese listed group in materials and packaging (cork). Scope: the consolidated IFRS statements for FY23 to FY25, from the 2025 and 2024 annual reports. Eleven subjects of the notes of the 2025 report were read. All four reading checks passed.

### Executive summary

1. **Revenue and EBITDA have fallen for two years (no rule fired).** Revenue was €985,467k in FY23, €939,061k in FY24 and €860,967k in FY25. EBITDA fell from €176,985k in FY23 to €140,983k in FY25.
2. **Cash generation swung from very weak to very strong (R10).** Cash conversion was 14.9%, 76.7% and 155.3% in the three years, as inventories fell. Over the three years together it was 77.0%.
3. **Debt was cut and refinanced, and note 22 shows how (R11).** Debt due within 12 months was 67.6% in FY23, 56.3% in FY24 and 28.7% in FY25. Adjusted net debt fell to €83,638k.
4. **Note 24 shows that "other financial liabilities" are mostly operating payables.** Of €43,264k current in 2025, the largest lines are sundry creditors, taxes and VAT. Repayable grants are the debt-like part.
5. **Other income is only partly explained, and investment runs below depreciation (R04, R24).** Of other income of €17,421k in FY25, note 30 leaves €7,051k as "Outros". Seven key documents are missing (R18).

### Information quality and limitations

The figures are the published consolidated accounts, in Portuguese. FY23 comes from the 2024 report; no line of FY24 differs between the two reports. The published EBITDA of FY25 is €140,985k; the lines read rebuild €140,983k, a rounding difference within the reading check tolerance. The notes were read from the printed text of the ESEF report, which has no page numbers. Year-end balances only.

### Next steps

1. Obtain monthly working capital balances and the inventory ageing by stage.
2. Obtain the facility agreements and the terms of the commercial paper programmes.
3. Obtain the ledger behind the "Outros" line of other income and the capex plan.
