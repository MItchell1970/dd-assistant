# Rules run — annual accounts alone against the whole messy data room (FICTITIOUS)

Before: the three annual statements only, as a listed company publishes them. After: the whole data room (ledger by month, bank statements, supplier listings, sales by customer, management adjustments, debt schedule).

**Before:** 22 of 32 rules run. **After:** 31 of 32 rules run. For reference, a listed company on its published accounts (TAP Air Portugal FY2025): 22 of 32 rules run, with R01, R02, R14, R16, R17, R20 blocked and R05, R06, R31, R32 not applicable.

| Rule | Before | After | What unblocks it |
|---|---|---|---|
| R01 · Customer concentration above threshold | blocked □ | run ✓ | Customer contract; monthly revenue and gross margin by customer |
| R02 · Material revenue from new customers | blocked □ | run ✓ | Customer contract and invoices; order book |
| R05 · Material management adjustments | blocked □ | run ✓ | Invoices and contracts supporting each adjustment |
| R06 · Recurring 'non-recurring' adjustment | blocked □ | run ✓ | Breakdown of the adjustment by year; plan and costs of the underlying programme |
| R16 · Sales by customer do not reconcile to P&L revenue | blocked □ | run ✓ | Sales-by-customer extract reconciled to the general ledger |
| R17 · Debt schedule does not reconcile to the balance sheet | blocked □ | run ✓ | Reconciled debt schedule; bank statements |
| R20 · Limited covenant headroom | blocked □ | run ✓ | Facility agreement; covenant compliance certificates |
| R31 · Cash not supported by the bank statements | blocked □ | run ✓ | Monthly bank reconciliations; general ledger detail of the cash accounts; bank confirmation letters |
| R32 · Supplier balances do not agree with the ledger | blocked □ | run ✓ | Reconciliation of the supplier sub-ledger to the general ledger; supplier statements of the largest suppliers |
