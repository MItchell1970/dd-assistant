# Manual time estimate — messy Duriva data room (FICTITIOUS)

**Written and committed before the agent's run**, so the comparison cannot be tuned afterwards (Phase 7 spec, B6).
The data room is fictitious: `data/Duriva_messy_data_room`, generated by `scripts/duriva_messy.py`.

**Source of the estimate.** No public study gives minutes per task for a first-pass financial due diligence. The rates
below are this project's hypothesis for one experienced associate in Excel, written per task so that each can be
challenged; Bernardo reviews them against his Transaction Services experience. They are deliberately the low end
(fast, experienced) so the comparison does not flatter the agent.

| # | Task | Units in this data room | Minutes per unit | Hypothesis behind the rate | Minutes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open and normalise the monthly ERP exports (three formats, header rows, number and date formats, a column named the wrong way round) | 35 files | 3 | Open, clean, paste into one sheet; the 2025 files need the swap spotted once, then repeated | 105 |
| 2 | Rebuild the missing month and link the renamed customer account | 2 issues | 15 | Notice the gap and the new code, work out the derivation, check it | 30 |
| 3 | Map the chart of accounts to the template lines | 36 accounts | 2 | Read the name, decide the line, note doubtful ones | 72 |
| 4 | Tie the mapped trial balances to the annual accounts (3 years) and investigate differences | 3 years | 20 | Build the tie-out, chase the residual to an account | 60 |
| 5 | Proof of cash: two banks, 36 months (statements in CSV and PDF) | 72 bank-months | 2 | Key the PDF lines or copy the CSV, monthly roll-forward, compare with the ledger | 144 |
| 6 | Receipts against revenue + VAT − change in receivables, by month | 36 months | 1.5 | One formula row per month once the data is in place | 54 |
| 7 | Supplier listings against the ledger | 2 year-ends | 10 | Sum the listing, compare, note the difference | 20 |
| 8 | Monthly working capital, LTM and a peg reference from 12–24 month-ends | 1 | 45 | Build the monthly table and the averages | 45 |
| 9 | Run the analytical tests (red flags) and write the findings with their source | 1 | 90 | The 32 tests of the rulebook done by hand on the mapped figures | 90 |
| | **Total** | | | | **620 minutes ≈ 10.3 hours** |

**What the agent's time includes** (measured, not estimated): the clock of the pipeline from the files to the
databook, plus the reviewer's time to decide the Low accounts and read the Medium ones and the proof of cash
differences. The reviewer's time is timed by a person; until it is, it is shown as "not yet timed", never assumed.
