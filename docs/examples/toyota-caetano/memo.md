# Toyota Caetano Portugal, S.A. — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · M&A buy-side · Amounts in EUR thousands (€k) · Sources: published consolidated accounts FY25 with FY24 comparatives (Sheet!Cell references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of Toyota Caetano Portugal, a Portuguese automotive importer and distributor, listed. Scope: the consolidated FY24 and FY25 statements of the 2025 annual report, under IFRS, in Portuguese. The cash flow statement uses the direct method. Both reading checks passed: operating profit is rebuilt to the euro.

### Executive summary

1. **Other income is a large part of EBITDA (R04).** Other income was €40,036k in FY25, 60.5% of EBITDA. Its content is not disclosed in the statements.
2. **Operating cash flow turned negative (R10).** Operating cash flow was -€3,987k in FY25, against €62,857k in FY24. Cash conversion over the two years together was 41.8%.
3. **Inventories grew far ahead of cost of sales (R09, R28).** Inventories rose 25.8% in FY25 against COGS up 4.5%. Net working capital rose to €101,201k.
4. **Investment is well below depreciation (R24).** Capex was 0.32x D&A in FY25 and 0.42x in FY24. Lower D&A, not EBITDA, lifted operating profit.
5. **Debt rose but leverage stays moderate; documents are missing (R18).** Adjusted net debt is €78,039k, 1.18x EBITDA. Seven key documents were not provided.

### Information quality and limitations

The figures are the published consolidated accounts, in Portuguese; the notes were not reviewed. Year-end balances only: there are no monthly figures. Sector benchmarks come from 22 European listed automotive retailers and are orientation only.

### Next steps

3. Obtain the capex plan and the fixed asset register.
