# Toyota Caetano Portugal, S.A. — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · M&A buy-side · Amounts in EUR thousands (€k) · Sources: published consolidated accounts FY23 to FY25 and the notes of the 2025 annual report (Sheet!Cell and note references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of Toyota Caetano Portugal, a listed Portuguese automotive importer and distributor. Scope: the consolidated IFRS statements for FY23 to FY25, from the 2025 and 2024 annual reports, in Portuguese. Twelve subjects of the notes of the 2025 report were read. All three reading checks passed.

### Executive summary

1. **Other income is most of EBITDA, and note 33 says what it is (R04).** Other income was €40,036k in FY25, 60.5% of EBITDA. Its largest line is recoveries of warranty and other operating costs, €13,977k; gains on asset disposals were €4,862k.
2. **EBITDA did not turn into cash in two years out of three (R10).** Operating cash flow was -€12,859k in FY23, €62,857k in FY24 and -€3,987k in FY25. Cash conversion over the three years together was 23.6%.
3. **Stock financing sits outside borrowings (R28, note 22).** Other payables include €48,078k owed to Toyota Kreditbank for vehicle purchases in 2025. This is not in net financial debt of €76,532k.
4. **Stock grew far ahead of cost of sales (R09).** Inventories rose 25.8% in FY25 against COGS up 4.5%. Net working capital rose to €101,201k.
5. **Investment is below depreciation, and D&A swung (R24, R27).** Capex / D&A was 0.62x in FY23, 0.42x in FY24 and 0.32x in FY25. Note 5 explains the FY24 peak in amortisation.

### Information quality and limitations

The figures are the published consolidated accounts, in Portuguese. FY23 comes from the 2024 report; no line of FY24 differs between the two reports. The notes were read from the printed text of the ESEF report, which has no page numbers. Year-end balances only.

2. Agree whether the stock financing of note 22 is debt-like, and obtain the financing agreements.
