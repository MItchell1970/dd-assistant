# Apple Inc. — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · Amounts in USD millions unless stated · Sources: published condensed statements FY24 (Sheet!Cell references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of Apple Inc., a technology hardware and electronics group. Scope: the unaudited condensed statements for FY23 and FY24 and the non-GAAP reconciliation. The income statement is presented by function, so EBITDA is operating income plus D&A from the cash flow statement. All six reading checks passed.

### Executive summary

1. **Year-end working capital grew ahead of activity (R07, R09).** Receivables rose 13.2% and inventories 15.1%, while revenue grew 2.0% and COGS fell 1.8%. DSO moved from 28.1 to 31.2 days.
2. **Net debt depends on how investments are treated.** Reported net debt is $76,686m; adjusted net debt is $41,458m after current marketable securities of $35,228m. Non-current securities of $91,479m remain to assess.
3. **EBITDA quality looks clean on the face of the accounts.** EBITDA is $134,661m (34.4% margin) with no management adjustments. Share-based compensation of $11,688m stays in EBITDA as a cost.
4. **A one-off tax charge sits below EBITDA.** The non-GAAP reconciliation removes $10,246m of tax from FY24 net income of $93,736m (Non-GAAP reconciliation!C6:C7).
5. **Cash definitions and documents limit verification (R13, R18).** FY23 cash differs by $772m between statements, and seven key documents are missing.

### Information quality and limitations

The statements are condensed and unaudited. There is no customer listing, debt schedule or covenant information. Year-end balances only: no monthly data. Benchmarks come from European listed peers and serve as orientation only.

### Next steps

1. Obtain the monthly working capital balances and the AR and inventory ageing.
2. Confirm the treatment of marketable securities and deferred revenue in net debt.
3. Reconcile cash, restricted cash and the State Aid escrow.
4. Request the audited annual report and the debt agreements.
