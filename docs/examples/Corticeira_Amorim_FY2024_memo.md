# Corticeira Amorim, S.G.P.S., S.A. — preliminary financial due diligence

Memo for the deal director · M&A buy-side · Amounts in EUR thousands · Sources: published consolidated accounts FY24 (Sheet!Cell references below)

## Page 1

### Context and scope

Preliminary buy-side review of Corticeira Amorim, a Portuguese listed group in materials and packaging (cork). Scope: the consolidated FY23 and FY24 statements, prepared under IFRS as a listed group. The cash flow statement uses the direct method. All four reading checks passed.

### Executive summary

1. **Cash conversion recovered, but FY23 shows how volatile it is (R10).** Operating cash was 14.9% of EBITDA in FY23 and 76.7% in FY24. Operating cash flow rose from €26,296k to €120,913k.
2. **Working capital is the defining feature of the business.** Net working capital is €543,919k, 57.9% of revenue. Inventories alone are €466,545k, or 384.6 days of cost of sales.
3. **Debt is short-dated, and cash in the cash flow statement is net of overdrafts (R11, R13).** 56.3% of gross debt of €272,323k falls due within 12 months. Adjusted net debt is €203,909k.
4. **EBITDA has no management adjustments, but includes other income (R04).** Reported EBITDA is €157,566k (16.8% margin), with other income of €17,849k inside it.
5. **Information gaps limit verification (R18).** Seven of ten key documents are missing, including the debt schedule and inventory ageing.

### Information quality and limitations

The statements are the published consolidated accounts; notes were not reviewed. There is no customer listing, debt schedule or facility agreement. Year-end balances only. Benchmarks come from 42 listed European packaging groups and serve as orientation only.

### Next steps

1. Obtain monthly working capital balances and the inventory ageing by stage.
2. Reconcile cash and overdrafts, and classify other financial liabilities.
3. Obtain the facility agreements and the breakdown of other income.
