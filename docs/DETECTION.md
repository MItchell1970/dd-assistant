# Detection benchmark by mutation

Generated by `scripts/detection_benchmark.py`. One known distortion at a time is planted in a copy of the published figures of the three demo companies (three years, each with its sector and country), and the engine is run. The expected rule of each mutation was written before the first run and no threshold was changed afterwards. The published files are never changed.

**Detected: 48 of 49 planted distortions (98%).** Missed: 1. Left out of the rate: 8 where the rule already fired on the real accounts, and 0 where the accounts lack the line. Other rules that newly fired alongside: 11. Controls below materiality by design: 6 of 6 raised no High or Medium finding.

What this measures: whether a distortion of this size, planted in real accounts, trips the rule built for it. What it does not measure: distortions nobody thought of, smaller ones, or several at once.

## History

- **First run, 11 November 2026:** 48 of 49 detected; 5 of 6 controls correct. Control C02 on Corticeira Amorim (closing cash off by €2k) raised R13 as Medium: the overdraft reconciliation was all-or-nothing, so a €2k error left the whole €29,451k difference unexplained.
- **12 November 2026, with the reviewer's approval:** R13 changed. A line that explains at least 90% of the cash difference now leaves only the remainder unexplained; the remainder still fires when above €1k. Rerun: 6 of 6 controls correct, the same 48 of 49.
- **Not changed:** R23 on TAP (M18). The rule compares DPO year on year, and TAP's real DPO fell 96 days, which hides 20 planted days. The planted amount is about €12m, below TAP's balance sheet materiality of €43m. It stays in the rate as a miss.

## Not detected

- **TAP Air Portugal, M18** (Trade payables 20 days of cost of sales higher): expected R23; R23 value -75.65 against threshold 15 days.
